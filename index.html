SitSitz

Palm up at your waist. Lift it to your chest.

DownPlatz

Flat hand, palm down. Lower it to the floor.

StayBleib

Open palm towards your dog, like a stop sign.

ComeHier

Arm out wide, then sweep the hand to your chest.
