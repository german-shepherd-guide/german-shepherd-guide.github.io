Hand-signal command poster

Pick the commands your household uses, set the cue words, and get one printable sheet with every hand signal drawn. Pin it on the fridge so everyone gives the same word and the same hand.

  • 12 commands
  • English or German cues
  • PDF in A4 or US Letter

Everything happens on this page: nothing is saved or sent. The signals are common training conventions. If your trainer uses different ones, follow your trainer and type your own words.

Notes on the sheet

How to use the poster

  1. One word, one hand

    Pick one word and one signal per command, and ask everyone at home to use the same pair.

  2. Say it once

    Give the cue once, help your dog get it right, then reward. The sheet shows the word and the hand together.

  3. Your words

    Every cue box can be edited, so the sheet can match the words your trainer or club already uses.
